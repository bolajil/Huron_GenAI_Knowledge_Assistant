## 1. Executive Summary

Huron enters 2026 at an inflection point. The healthcare and higher education sectors — our core markets — are under simultaneous pressure from rising costs, workforce shortages, regulatory complexity, and accelerating AI adoption. Our 2026 strategy positions Huron at the intersection of operational transformation and AI enablement, capitalizing on our domain expertise with new technology offerings.

**Strategic Theme for 2026: "Intelligent Operations"**

## 4. Strategic Priorities

### Priority 1: AI-Enabled Consulting Delivery
Deploy generative AI tools across all service lines to improve analyst productivity, accelerate insight generation, and differentiate our deliverables. The Huron GenAI Knowledge Assistant is the first internal deployment — rolling out firm-wide Q1 2026.

- **Target:** 30% reduction in research/synthesis time by Q3 2026
- **Owner:** Chief Digital Officer
- **KPI:** # of engagements using AI-augmented delivery / client satisfaction scores

### Priority 2: Talent Density Over Headcount
After three years of rapid hiring, 2026 is a "quality year." We are investing in training, certifications, and performance differentiation rather than headcount growth.

- **Target:** 90th-percentile talent retention in senior ranks; promotion rate for high-performers ≥15%
- **Owner:** Chief People Officer
- **KPI:** Voluntary attrition rate; engagement survey scores; billability rates

### Priority 3: Expand Life Sciences Practice
Life sciences represents the fastest-growing segment by margin. We will develop three new service offerings: regulatory submission support, commercial launch strategy, and real-world evidence analytics.

- **Target:** Life sciences revenue to $120M by year-end 2026
- **Owner:** Life Sciences Practice Leader
- **KPI:** Revenue, pipeline, and new logo count

### Priority 4: Strengthen Recurring Revenue
Professional services revenue is project-based and lumpy. We are building subscription and managed service offerings — beginning with a Managed Analytics platform launching Q3 2026.

- **Target:** 10% of revenue from recurring/retainer arrangements by Q4 2026
- **Owner:** Chief Revenue Officer
- **KPI:** ARR from managed services; client retention on retainer contracts
