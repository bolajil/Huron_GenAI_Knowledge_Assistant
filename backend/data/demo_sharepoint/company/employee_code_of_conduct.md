## 1. Introduction

At Huron, our reputation is our most important asset. Every day, clients trust us to act with integrity, treat their people with respect, and protect their confidential information. This Code of Conduct defines the behaviors and ethical standards that make that trust possible.

This Code applies to all Huron employees, officers, contractors, and business partners acting on Huron's behalf, in all geographies and service lines.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest exists when personal, financial, or other relationships could impair — or appear to impair — your professional judgment.

### Examples That Must Be Disclosed
- Owning more than 1% of equity in a current or prospective client
- A family member employed by a client or competitor
- Serving on the board of a client organization
- Receiving gifts, meals, or travel from a client or vendor over $50 in value

### Disclosure Process
Submit a Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form via the Ethics portal (ethics.huron-demo.ai) within 5 business days of becoming aware of a potential conflict. Huron Legal and your HR Business Partner will review and advise on next steps.

## 4. Gifts & Entertainment

| Scenario | Acceptable? |
|---|---|
| Client dinner (business purpose, under $150/person) | Yes, with manager approval |
| Ticket to sporting event with client (under $250 value) | Yes, with Director approval |
| Cash gift from any external party | Never |
| Gift card from vendor | Never |
| Holiday gift basket from vendor (under $50) | Yes, must be shared with team |
| Travel or accommodations paid by vendor | Never without written VP + Legal approval |

Never accept or give gifts that could influence a business decision or create an appearance of impropriety. When in doubt, decline and report to your HR Business Partner.

## 5. Workplace Conduct

### Harassment & Discrimination
Huron is committed to a workplace free from harassment and discrimination based on race, color, sex, national origin, religion, age (40+), disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, genetic information, pregnancy, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

Harassment includes verbal abuse, offensive jokes, threats, unwelcome physical contact, or conduct that creates an intimidating or hostile work environment.

**All harassment incidents must be reported.** You can report to your manager, HR, or anonymously via the Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-0199.

### Violence & Threatening Behavior
Zero tolerance. Any credible threat of violence is grounds for immediate termination and referral to law enforcement.

### Substance Use
Employees must report to work unimpaired. Use of alcohol or controlled substances on Huron premises or while performing Huron work is prohibited. Use of legally prescribed medication that may impair judgment must be disclosed to HR.

---

## 6. Protecting Company & Client Information

- All client information is confidential. Do not discuss client matters in public places.
- Never share confidential information on personal devices or personal cloud accounts
- Huron intellectual property (methodologies, frameworks, deliverables) must be protected; do not share externally without client authorization and Legal review
- Social media: Do not disclose client names, engagement details, or Huron financial information. All external statements on Huron's behalf must be approved by Corporate Communications

---

## 7. Anti-Corruption & Anti-Bribery

Huron complies with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA), the UK Bribery Act, and applicable local anti-corruption laws.

**Never offer, pay, promise, or authorize** a payment (money, gifts, services, or anything of value) to:
- A government official to obtain or retain business
- Any party to influence a decision in Huron's favor

This prohibition applies to payments made directly or indirectly through third parties.

---

## 8. Reporting Violations

### Who to Contact
| Situation | Contact |
|---|---|
| HR/workplace conduct issues | HR Business Partner |
| Financial irregularities | CFO or Internal Audit |
| Legal/compliance concerns | General Counsel |
| Any ethics matter, including anonymous reports | Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-0199 |

### Non-Retaliation
Huron strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith. Retaliation is itself a violation of this Code and grounds for disciplinary action up to and including termination.

---

## 9. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this Code may result in:
- Verbal or written warning
- Mandatory ethics training
- Suspension without pay
- Termination of employment
- Referral to law enforcement (for criminal conduct)

Severity of consequences depends on the nature, intent, and impact of the violation.
