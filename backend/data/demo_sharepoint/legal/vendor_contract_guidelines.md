# Vendor Contract Guidelines (Demo Document)
**Version:** 2026.1 | **Classification:** Internal | **Owner:** Legal

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes standards for negotiating, reviewing, and executing contracts with external vendors, suppliers, and third-party service providers. All vendor contracts must comply with these guidelines before execution.

---

## 2. Contract Authority Thresholds

No employee may commit Huron to a vendor contract without appropriate authority:

| Contract Value (Annual) | Signing Authority |
|---|---|
| Under $10,000 | Department Manager |
| $10,000 – $50,000 | VP or Senior Director |
| $50,000 – $250,000 | C-Suite Officer |
| Over $250,000 | CFO + CEO (dual signature required) |
| Multi-year commitments | Add one tier above for each additional year |

**Legal review is required for all contracts over $25,000** regardless of signing authority.

---

## 3. Required Contract Terms

Every vendor contract must include the following provisions. If the vendor's paper does not include them, Huron Legal must negotiate their inclusion or issue a written exception.

### 3.1 Data Protection
- Specification of data types shared with the vendor
- Vendor's obligation to process data only per Huron's instructions
- Data residency requirements (US-only by default; exceptions require CISO approval)
- Data breach notification within **48 hours** of discovery
- Right to audit vendor's security controls annually

### 3.2 Confidentiality
- Mutual NDA terms with a minimum 3-year post-termination survival period
- Definition of confidential information must include Huron client data and business strategies

### 3.3 Liability & Insurance
| Coverage Type | Minimum Required |
|---|---|
| Commercial General Liability | $2M per occurrence / $4M aggregate |
| Professional Liability (E&O) | $1M per occurrence |
| Cyber Liability | $2M per occurrence (if vendor handles data) |
| Workers Compensation | Per state statutory limits |

### 3.4 Termination Rights
- Termination for convenience (Huron) with **30 days written notice** (standard)
- Immediate termination for material breach (with 10-day cure period)
- Automatic termination triggers: vendor insolvency, data breach, regulatory sanction

### 3.5 SLA / Performance Standards
- Define measurable KPIs and uptime commitments
- Service credits for missed SLAs (minimum 10% monthly fee credit per missed month)
- Escalation path for disputes

## 4. Procurement Process

### Step 1: Business Need & Vendor Selection
- Submit a vendor request form to Procurement (procurement@huron-demo.ai)
- Competitive bids required for contracts over $25,000 (minimum 3 bids)
- Sole-source justification form required if competitive bidding is waived

### Step 2: Vendor Due Diligence
Finance runs credit and sanctions screening on all new vendors. IT Security runs a security questionnaire for any vendor with system access or data handling. Results must be documented in Huron's vendor registry.

### Step 3: Contract Review
- Under $25K: Procurement reviews against this policy checklist
- Over $25K: Mandatory Legal review (target 5 business days for standard agreements)
- Complex agreements (IP licensing, data processing agreements): Legal review up to 15 business days

### Step 4: Execution
- Use DocuSign for all contract execution
- Contracts are stored in the Huron contract repository (SharePoint: Legal → Contracts)
- Do not execute via personal email or unsecured file sharing

---

## 5. Vendor Risk Classification

| Risk Tier | Definition | Review Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 – Critical | Single-source dependency or handles sensitive data | Annual security audit + quarterly business review |
| Tier 2 – High | Significant spend or material service impact | Annual review |
| Tier 3 – Standard | All other vendors | Biennial review |

---

## 6. Prohibited Practices

- No side agreements, letters of intent, or verbal commitments that create financial obligations
- Do not allow vendor access to Huron systems before IT approval and contract execution
- No acceptance of vendor terms-of-service for SaaS tools without Legal review if cost exceeds $5,000/year
- No sharing of Huron client data with vendors until a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is signed

---

## 7. Renewals & Amendments

- Set renewal reminders at 90 days before expiration (vendor management team manages this)
- Auto-renewing contracts must be flagged in the contract repository; Legal will alert the business owner 90 days in advance
- Any amendment to a signed contract follows the same approval thresholds as the original
