# Huron Data Privacy Policy (Demo Document)
**Version:** 2026.1 | **Classification:** Internal | **Owner:** Legal — Privacy

---

## 1. Purpose

Huron Consulting Group is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all personal and confidential data processed in connection with client engagements, employee records, and internal operations. This policy establishes standards for collection, use, storage, and disposal of personal data in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, and applicable state privacy laws.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All personal data collected from or about Huron employees
- All personal data collected from clients, prospects, and business contacts
- All client engagement data containing personal information
- Any third party processing data on Huron's behalf (vendors, subprocessors)

---

## 3. Data Classification & Handling

| Level | Examples | Handling Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Marketing materials, press releases, published case studies | No restrictions |
| **Internal** | Org charts, internal process docs, meeting notes | Internal distribution only; do not share externally |
| **Confidential** | Client data, financials, strategy documents, proposals | Need-to-know; encrypted at rest and in transit |
| **Restricted** | PII, PHI, legal holds, credentials, employee health data | Strict access controls; access logged; minimum necessary principle |

When in doubt, classify at the next higher level.

---

## 4. Data Collection Principles

Huron collects only the personal data necessary for a specified, legitimate purpose (data minimization). Before collecting any new category of personal data, teams must:

1. Document the purpose of collection
2. Identify the legal basis (consent, contract, legitimate interest, legal obligation)
3. Notify affected individuals via appropriate privacy notice
4. Obtain Privacy Officer sign-off if collecting Restricted data or health information

---

## 5. Data Retention Schedule

| Data Category | Retention Period | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Client engagement files | 7 years after project close | Contractual + regulatory |
| Client PHI (HIPAA) | Per BAA terms; minimum 6 years from creation | HIPAA 45 CFR §164.530(j) |
| Employee records (general) | Duration of employment + 7 years | Employment law |
| Employee health / benefits records | Duration of employment + 6 years | HIPAA / ERISA |
| Financial records (AR/AP, general ledger) | 10 years | Tax / SOX |
| Tax records | 7 years | IRS |
| Email correspondence | 3 years active, then 4-year archive (7 total) | Legal hold policy |
| Security logs / access logs | 1 year active, 2 years archive | Security policy |
| Recruitment / candidate data (not hired) | 2 years | EEOC guidance |

Records under a legal hold are retained until the hold is released regardless of the above schedule.

## 6. GDPR Compliance (EU/UK Data Subjects)

Huron processes EU/UK personal data as both a Controller (employee data) and Processor (client engagement data).

### Legal Bases for Processing
- **Contract performance:** Processing employee or client contact data necessary to deliver services
- **Legitimate interest:** Business analytics, fraud prevention, service improvement
- **Legal obligation:** Tax, employment, and regulatory compliance
- **Consent:** Marketing emails, optional product features

### Data Subject Rights
Data subjects (employees, clients, business contacts) have the right to:

| Right | Huron Response Time |
|---|---|
| Access (Subject Access Request — SAR) | Within 30 days (extensible to 90 days for complex requests) |
| Rectification | Within 30 days |
| Erasure ("right to be forgotten") | Within 45 days; pending legal hold review |
| Data portability | Within 30 days; machine-readable format (CSV/JSON) |
| Restriction of processing | Within 5 business days (processing suspended pending review) |
| Object to processing | Immediate suspension of marketing; other objections reviewed within 30 days |

Submit requests to: privacy@huron-demo.ai

### International Transfers
Transfers of EU/UK personal data outside the EEA require a lawful transfer mechanism:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) — Huron's default for vendor agreements
- UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) for UK data
- Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) — not currently in place
- Adequacy decision — applies to transfers to US (limited to Data Privacy Framework participants)

---

## 7. CCPA Compliance (California Residents)

California residents have the following rights:
- **Know:** What personal information is collected and how it's used
- **Delete:** Request deletion of personal information (subject to exceptions)
- **Correct:** Request correction of inaccurate personal information
- **Opt-out:** Of sale or sharing of personal information (Huron does not sell data)
- **Non-discrimination:** No penalty for exercising privacy rights

Response time: 45 days (extensible to 90 days with notice).

---

## 8. Data Breach Response

### Internal Detection → Reporting
Any employee who discovers or suspects a data breach must:
1. Immediately contact the Privacy Officer: privacy@huron-demo.ai or x4800
2. Preserve evidence — do not delete any files, emails, or logs
3. Do not attempt to investigate or remediate independently

### Notification Timelines
| Party | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Huron Privacy Officer | Within 1 hour |
| Huron Legal | Within 4 hours |
| Affected Client (per BAA/contract) | Within 24–48 hours |
| EU Supervisory Authority (if EU data involved) | Within 72 hours of discovery |
| Affected individuals (if high risk) | Without undue delay |
| HHS (HIPAA breach ≥500 individuals) | Within 60 days |
| State AG (varies by state) | Per state breach law (30–90 days) |

---

## 9. Third-Party Data Processing

Vendors that process personal data on Huron's behalf must:
- Sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before receiving any personal data
- Implement technical and organizational security measures equivalent to Huron's standards
- Notify Huron of any breach within 48 hours
- Process data only per Huron's written instructions
- Delete or return all personal data upon contract termination

A list of Huron's approved data subprocessors is maintained by Legal and reviewed quarterly.

---

## 10. Privacy by Design

New systems, tools, or data collection initiatives must undergo a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) if they:
- Introduce a new category of personal data collection
- Change how existing personal data is used or shared
- Involve processing of Restricted data at scale

PIA forms are submitted to privacy@huron-demo.ai. Privacy Officer review target: 10 business days.
