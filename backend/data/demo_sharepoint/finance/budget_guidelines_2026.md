**Version:** 2026.1 | **Classification:** Internal — Confidential | **Owner:** Finance

## 1. Overview

These guidelines govern budget planning, approval, and management for FY2026 (January 1 – December 31, 2026). All practice leaders and department heads are responsible for ensuring their teams operate within approved budgets.

FY2026 corporate targets: 10% revenue growth to $1.12B; Adjusted EBITDA margin of 15.5%.

## 3. Headcount Planning

### Replacement Hires
Replacement hires for roles vacated within the same quarter are **automatically approved** (Finance budget is maintained). The hiring manager submits a standard job requisition through Workday.

### Net-New Headcount
Net-new positions require:
1. Submission of a Business Case Form to Finance (finance@huron-demo.ai) by the October 15 deadline
2. SVP-level approval
3. Headcount must be included in the approved FY2026 budget — mid-year net-new requests require CFO approval

### Contractor and Contingent Workforce
- Contractors over 90 days (or over $100K annual cost) require VP Finance approval
- All contractors must be onboarded through Procurement — no direct personal service contracts

## 5. Capital Expenditure (CapEx) Thresholds

Items with a useful life of more than 1 year and a cost above $2,500 are capitalized rather than expensed.

| CapEx Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Under $5,000 | Director |
| $5,000 – $25,000 | VP |
| $25,000 – $100,000 | SVP + VP Finance |
| Over $100,000 | CFO + CEO |

CapEx requests must include: description, total cost, useful life estimate, vendor quote, and ROI or strategic justification.

---

## 6. Operating Expense (OpEx) Authority

| OpEx Amount (single purchase or annual commitment) | Approval Chain |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Direct manager |
| $500 – $2,500 | Manager + Finance notification |
| $2,500 – $10,000 | Director + Finance approval |
| $10,000 – $50,000 | VP + Finance |
| Over $50,000 | SVP + CFO |

**Subscriptions and recurring services:** The annual total value of a subscription determines the approval tier. Multi-year commitments escalate one tier above the annual cost tier.

---

## 7. Budget Variance Policy

Departments must operate within ±5% of their approved quarterly budget. Variances exceeding this threshold require:
- Explanation memo to Finance within 10 business days of month-end close
- Reforecast submission if the variance is expected to persist beyond one quarter

Favorable variances (underspend) may not be automatically reallocated. Budget transfers between cost centers require VP Finance approval.

---

## 8. Reporting & Monitoring

### Monthly Budget Reports
Finance distributes monthly budget-vs-actual reports by the 10th business day of the following month. Reports are available in SharePoint → Finance → Monthly Reports.

### Practice P&L Reviews
Each practice leader participates in a monthly P&L review call with Finance. Format:
- Revenue: billed vs. backlog vs. pipeline
- Headcount: actual vs. budget vs. open reqs
- Direct costs: project expenses vs. budget
- Gross margin vs. target

### Cost Allocation
- Shared services (IT, HR, Legal, Finance) are allocated to practices on a headcount-weighted basis
- Facility costs allocated on a square footage basis
- Corporate overhead not allocated below the company level

---

## 9. Procurement Rules

- All vendor contracts over $25,000 require Legal review before signing
- Competitive bids (minimum 3) required for purchases over $25,000 (see Vendor Contract Guidelines)
- No commitment of company funds may be made without proper approval per §6 above
- Never sign vendor contracts in a personal capacity — all contracts must be executed through Legal/Procurement
