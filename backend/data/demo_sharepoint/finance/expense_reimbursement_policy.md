# Expense Reimbursement Policy (Demo Document)
**Version:** 2026.1 | **Classification:** Internal | **Owner:** Finance

---

## 1. Overview

This policy governs the submission, approval, and reimbursement of business expenses incurred by Huron employees. All expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to Huron business activities. Reimbursement is not guaranteed for expenses that violate this policy.

---

## 2. Expense Submission Deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the date will not be reimbursed except with written VP Finance approval.

Submit expense reports through the Concur Expense portal (concur.huron-demo.ai).

---

## 3. Travel Expenses

### Air Travel
- Book through the Huron travel portal (Concur Travel) at least **7 days in advance** where possible
- Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours
- Business class is permitted for international flights over 6 hours with manager pre-approval
- Personal credit card bookings outside the portal must include a written justification

### Ground Transportation
| Type | Policy |
|---|---|
| Taxi / Rideshare | Reimbursable; use Huron corporate Uber/Lyft account when possible |
| Personal vehicle mileage | Reimbursed at IRS rate (currently $0.67/mile); submit mileage log |
| Rental car | Compact or mid-size only; decline optional insurance (Huron card covers it) |
| Parking | Reimbursable with receipt; valet not covered unless no self-park available |

### Hotel
- Standard rooms only; no suites or upgrades at company expense
- Per-diem hotel rate limits by city:
  | City | Max Nightly Rate |
  |---|---|
  | Chicago, New York, San Francisco | $300 |
  | Boston, Seattle, Miami | $250 |
  | All other US cities | $200 |

- Rates exceeding limits require manager pre-approval
- Personal charges (movies, minibar, spa) are not reimbursable

### Per Diem — Meals
| Location | Daily Meal Allowance |
|---|---|
| High-cost cities (NYC, SF, Chicago) | $80/day |
| Standard US cities | $60/day |
| International (varies by country) | Per GSA international per-diem table |

Receipts are required for meal expenses over $25. Client-entertainment meals follow separate Entertainment Policy rules.

---

## 4. Client Entertainment

Client entertainment expenses require **pre-approval** from a Director or above before the event.

| Type | Limit Per Person | Approval Required |
|---|---|---|
| Client meals | $150 | Manager |
| Client events / tickets | $250 | Director |
| Client gifts | $50 | Director (one gift per client per year) |
| Client golf / recreational | $200 | VP |

The business purpose, client name, and attendee list must be documented on the expense report.

### Restricted Entertainment
The following may not be expensed regardless of business purpose:
- Entertainment at adult establishments
- Political donations or fundraiser events
- Personal fines or traffic violations
- Gifts to government officials (per anti-bribery policy)

---

## 5. Technology & Equipment

| Item | Limit | Process |
|---|---|---|
| Home office equipment (single purchase) | $500 | Manager approval |
| Home office equipment (annual) | $1,000 | Manager approval |
| Software / subscriptions | $200/month | IT approval required |
| Cell phone usage (work portion) | $75/month | Automatic with enrollment |

All equipment purchased with company funds is Huron property and must be returned upon separation.

---

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are **never** reimbursable:
- Commuting costs between home and the primary office
- Personal entertainment or family travel
- Clothing or personal care (unless required for client event — submit for pre-approval)
- Traffic and parking violations
- Expenses already reimbursed by a client
- Expenses incurred by family members traveling with the employee
- Alcoholic beverages (except as part of approved client entertainment)

---

## 7. Approval & Payment

| Expense Amount | Approval Chain |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Direct manager |
| $500 – $2,500 | Manager + Finance |
| Over $2,500 | VP + Finance + CFO |

Approved expense reports are processed within **7 business days** of approval. Reimbursements are paid via direct deposit to the employee's bank account on record.
