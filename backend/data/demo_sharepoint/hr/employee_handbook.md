## 1. Welcome to Huron

Huron Consulting Group is a professional services firm helping organizations achieve transformational change. Our mission is to deliver sustainable outcomes by partnering with clients to drive growth, improve performance, and navigate complex challenges.

This handbook applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees in the United States.

---

## 2. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to uphold Huron's core values:
- **Integrity** — Act honestly and transparently in all interactions
- **Respect** — Foster an inclusive, psychologically safe environment
- **Collaboration** — Work across teams and functions to deliver the best outcomes
- **Excellence** — Hold ourselves accountable to the highest professional standards

Violations of the Code of Conduct should be reported to your HR Business Partner or via the anonymous Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199.

---

## 3. Compensation & Benefits

### Salary Reviews
Compensation is reviewed annually each December. Merit increases are based on performance ratings from the mid-year and year-end review cycles.

### Benefits Package
| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Medical | Blue Cross PPO and HDHP options; employer covers 80% of premium |
| Dental | Delta Dental — preventive 100%, basic 80%, major 50% |
| Vision | VSP — annual exam and $200 frame allowance |
| 401(k) | 4% employer match; vesting begins at 12 months |
| Life Insurance | 2x annual salary, company-paid |
| PTO | 20 days (years 0–2), 25 days (years 3–6), 30 days (7+) |
| Sick Leave | 10 days per year, non-accruing |
| Parental Leave | 16 weeks fully paid (primary caregiver), 6 weeks (secondary) |

### Professional Development
- $2,500 annual learning budget per employee
- Eligible for tuition reimbursement up to $5,250/year after 1 year of service
- All employees must complete 40 hours of CPE annually

## 4. Work Arrangements

### In-Office Expectation
Employees are expected in the office Tuesday through Thursday. Monday and Friday are flexible remote days unless client commitments require otherwise.

### Full-Remote Roles
Full-remote arrangements require VP-level approval and are reviewed annually. Remote employees must maintain a dedicated, secure workspace and attend in-person team events quarterly.

### Working Hours
Core hours are 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time. Outside of core hours, employees manage their own schedules in coordination with their teams.

---

## 5. Performance Management

### Review Cycle
- **Mid-Year Review (June):** Feedback and goal check-in; no compensation changes
- **Year-End Review (December):** Formal rating; drives compensation adjustments and promotion decisions

### Rating Scale
| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 5 – Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all expectations |
| 4 – Exceeds Expectations | Regularly surpasses goals |
| 3 – Meets Expectations | Reliably delivers on commitments |
| 2 – Needs Improvement | Falls short in key areas; improvement plan required |
| 1 – Unsatisfactory | Significant gaps; may result in separation |

---

## 6. Separation & Offboarding

Employees leaving Huron must:
1. Submit resignation in writing to their manager and HR (minimum 2 weeks notice)
2. Return all company equipment within 5 business days of last day
3. Complete the IT offboarding checklist to revoke system access
4. Participate in an exit interview with HR

Final paychecks are issued on the next regular pay date following the last day of work.
