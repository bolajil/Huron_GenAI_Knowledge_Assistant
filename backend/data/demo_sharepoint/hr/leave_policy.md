# Huron Leave Policy (Demo Document)
**Version:** 2026.1 | **Classification:** Internal | **Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Overview

This policy outlines all leave types available to Huron employees, eligibility criteria, and the process for requesting leave. All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance, except for emergency situations.

---

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Accrual Schedule
| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days | Accrual Rate (per pay period) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 2 years | 20 days | 0.77 days |
| 3 – 6 years | 25 days | 0.96 days |
| 7+ years | 30 days | 1.15 days |

### Rules
- PTO begins accruing on the first day of employment
- Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days to the following calendar year
- PTO balances above 5 days do not carry over and are forfeited on December 31
- Upon separation, unused accrued PTO is paid out per applicable state law
- PTO may not be taken in advance of accrual (negative PTO balance not permitted)

---

## 3. Sick Leave

- **Allowance:** 10 days per calendar year (non-accruing, resets January 1)
- **Use:** Illness, injury, medical appointments, or caring for an ill immediate family member
- **Doctor's Note:** Required after 3 consecutive sick days
- **Unused Balance:** Not paid out on separation

Sick leave runs concurrently with FMLA where eligible.

---

## 4. Parental Leave

### Primary Caregiver
- **Duration:** 16 weeks fully paid
- **Eligibility:** Employees with 6+ months of service at time of birth/adoption
- **Trigger:** Birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child

### Secondary Caregiver
- **Duration:** 6 weeks fully paid
- **Eligibility:** Same as primary caregiver

### Usage Rules
- Must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event
- May be taken in one continuous block or in two blocks (minimum 2 weeks each)
- Notify HR at least 30 days in advance (or as soon as practicable)

---

## 5. Bereavement Leave

| Relationship | Paid Leave |
|---|---|
| Spouse / domestic partner | 5 days |
| Child (including step, adopted, or foster) | 5 days |
| Parent (including step or in-law) | 3 days |
| Sibling, grandparent, grandchild | 3 days |
| Close personal friend or extended family | 1 day (manager discretion) |

Additional unpaid leave may be approved by the HR Business Partner for circumstances not covered above.

---

## 6. FMLA (Family and Medical Leave Act)

Employees who have worked at Huron for 12+ months and logged 1,250+ hours in the prior 12-month period are eligible for up to **12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave** per year for:

- Serious health condition (self)
- Care for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition
- Birth/adoption/foster placement of a child
- Qualifying military exigency

To initiate FMLA, contact HR to receive the FMLA Notice of Eligibility form. Medical certification must be returned within 15 days.

## 7. Other Leave Types

| Leave Type | Duration | Pay Status |
|---|---|---|
| Jury Duty | Duration of service | Paid (up to 10 days) |
| Witness Subpoena | Duration required | Paid |
| Military Leave | Per USERRA | Unpaid (up to 5 years protected) |
| Voting Leave | Up to 2 hours | Paid |
| Volunteer Emergency Responder | Up to 14 days/year | Unpaid |

---

## 8. How to Request Leave

1. Log in to the HR portal (hr.huron-demo.ai)
2. Select **My Time** → **Request Leave**
3. Choose leave type, dates, and add supporting documentation
4. Your manager will be notified automatically and must approve within 3 business days
5. HR reviews and confirms FMLA/medical leaves within 5 business days

For emergency leave, notify your manager by phone immediately, then submit the portal request within 2 business days.
