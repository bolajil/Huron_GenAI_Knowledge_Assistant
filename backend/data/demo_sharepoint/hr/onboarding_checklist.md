# New Employee Onboarding Checklist (Demo Document)
**Version:** 2026.1 | **Classification:** Internal | **Owner:** Human Resources

---

## Before Your First Day (HR & Manager Actions)

- [ ] Offer letter countersigned and filed in Workday
- [ ] Background check cleared
- [ ] IT equipment ordered (laptop, monitor, peripherals)
- [ ] System access provisioned (Workday, Microsoft 365, SharePoint, VPN)
- [ ] First-day schedule sent to new hire
- [ ] New hire added to team Slack/Teams channels
- [ ] Buddy assigned (same practice; 2+ years tenure)
- [ ] Office badge access configured

---

## Day 1 — Welcome & Administration

### New Hire Actions
- [ ] Complete I-9 and W-4 forms (HR portal — must be completed by EOD)
- [ ] Receive IT equipment and verify access credentials (laptop, email, VPN)
- [ ] Meet with HR Business Partner (30-min welcome call)
- [ ] Meet your assigned buddy
- [ ] Complete Security Awareness Training (IT portal — 45 min, due within 5 days)
- [ ] Review and acknowledge Code of Conduct (HR portal)
- [ ] Set up Microsoft Authenticator app for MFA
- [ ] Take a photo for your Huron ID badge and Workday profile

### Manager Actions
- [ ] Give team introduction (all-team meeting or Slack introduction)
- [ ] Walk through 30/60/90-day expectations in writing
- [ ] Share team norms document (meeting cadence, Slack etiquette, how work gets reviewed)
- [ ] Confirm buddy intro has happened

---

## Week 1 — Getting Started

- [ ] Complete all required compliance training modules:
  - [ ] HIPAA Awareness (if in Clinical practice) — 60 min
  - [ ] Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption — 30 min
  - [ ] Workplace Harassment Prevention — 45 min
  - [ ] Data Privacy Fundamentals — 30 min
- [ ] Shadow team members for 2–3 key workflows relevant to your role
- [ ] Set up recurring 1:1 cadence with direct manager (weekly recommended for first 90 days)
- [ ] Review department goals and OKRs for current quarter
- [ ] Explore Huron intranet and SharePoint knowledge base
- [ ] Request access to role-specific systems (submit ServiceNow IT ticket)
- [ ] Introduce yourself in your practice's Slack/Teams channel

---

## Week 2–4 — Settling In

- [ ] Enroll in benefits **(deadline: 30 days from hire date — no exceptions)**
  - [ ] Medical, dental, vision elections in Workday
  - [ ] 401(k) enrollment and contribution rate set
  - [ ] Beneficiary designations completed
  - [ ] Life insurance and disability elections confirmed
- [ ] Complete CPE/continuing education plan for current year (if applicable to your role)
- [ ] Attend first Practice all-hands meeting
- [ ] Connect with 3–5 cross-practice colleagues (use the New Hire Coffee Chat program — sign up in HR portal)

---

## 30-Day Checkpoint

- [ ] Submit 30-day check-in survey to HR (emailed automatically on Day 28)
- [ ] 30-day 1:1 with manager: review expectations, discuss any blockers
- [ ] Confirm all compliance training completed (HR will flag if anything is missing)
- [ ] Expense account and corporate card activated (if applicable)
- [ ] Travel profile set up in Concur Travel

---

## 60-Day Checkpoint

- [ ] 60-day 1:1 with manager: performance check-in, goal refinement
- [ ] Complete any role-specific certifications required (see Role Requirements in Workday)
- [ ] Introduced to at least 2 client teams or internal projects
- [ ] Performance goals formally documented in Workday (must be done before 90-day mark)

---

## 90-Day Checkpoint

- [ ] 90-day formal check-in with manager and HR Business Partner
- [ ] Goals set in Workday performance module for current review cycle
- [ ] Any remaining access requests resolved
- [ ] Buddy relationship continues informally; formal check-ins optional after 90 days
- [ ] Complete New Hire Experience survey (HR portal)
