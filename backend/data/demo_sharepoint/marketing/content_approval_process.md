# Content Approval & Publishing Process (Demo Document)
**Version:** 2026.1 | **Classification:** Internal | **Owner:** Marketing

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how Huron reviews, approves, and publishes external content — including thought leadership articles, blog posts, social media, event materials, and press releases. A consistent approval process protects the Huron brand, ensures legal compliance, and maintains quality across all public-facing channels.

## 3. Submission Process

### Step 1: Draft
Author completes a draft and self-reviews against:
- Brand voice guidelines (see Brand Guidelines doc)
- Accuracy of all data and statistics (cite sources)
- No mention of specific client names without prior written consent
- No forward-looking financial statements
- No regulatory claims without Legal pre-review

### Step 2: Submit via Content Portal
Submit via the Content Submission Form (SharePoint → Marketing → Submit Content):
- Attach the draft document
- Select content category and intended publication channel
- Provide target publication date
- Note any time sensitivity or client sensitivity flags

### Step 3: Practice Leader Review
For technical content, the relevant Practice Leader (or delegate) verifies:
- Subject matter accuracy and currency
- No disclosure of proprietary client methodologies without approval
- Alignment with current practice positioning

Turnaround: 3 business days. If Practice Leader does not respond within 3 days, escalate to their EA.

### Step 4: Communications Review
Corporate Communications reviews for:
- Brand voice and tone alignment
- Headline and subheading effectiveness
- Consistency with current messaging frameworks
- Overall editorial quality

Turnaround: 3 business days. Communications may request revisions — revised drafts restart the Communications review clock only (not the full process).

### Step 5: Legal Review (conditional)
Required when content:
- References regulatory matters (HIPAA, CMS, FDA, FERPA)
- Includes client-specific claims or results
- Could be read as making healthcare/financial advice
- Involves any pending litigation or regulatory investigation topic

Legal turnaround: 3–5 business days. Legal may clear as-is, require changes, or block publication.

### Step 6: Final Approval & Scheduling
VP Communications issues final approval in the Content Portal (status → Approved). Content is scheduled for publication per the editorial calendar.

## 4. Social Media Policy (Employee Guidelines)

Huron employees are encouraged to be thought leaders on LinkedIn. When posting in a professional capacity:

### Encouraged
- Sharing published Huron content (blog posts, press releases, white papers)
- Posting insights from client engagements (without identifying the client)
- Engaging with industry news and trends
- Celebrating team wins and firm milestones

### Required Disclosures
If your post could be read as representing Huron's official position, add: *"Views expressed are my own."*

### Prohibited
- Identifying clients by name without written consent (even positive references)
- Sharing non-public financial information (revenue figures, deal sizes)
- Commenting on ongoing litigation or regulatory proceedings
- Posting confidential proposal or pricing information
- Speaking on behalf of Huron on political matters

### Responding to Negative Comments
Do not respond to negative comments about Huron, clients, or competitors on social media. Forward the comment to Communications at comms@huron-demo.ai and let them respond.

## 5. Campaign Planning

### Annual Campaign Calendar
The Marketing team publishes an annual editorial and campaign calendar each January. Practice leaders nominate content themes by November 30 for the following year. Calendar is published in SharePoint → Marketing → Campaign Calendar.

### Quarterly Planning
Each quarter, Marketing holds a Content Planning session with Practice Leaders (first Thursday of the last month of each quarter) to:
- Review pipeline of approved content
- Confirm conference and event speaking slots
- Prioritize thought leadership by market priority

### Urgent / Out-of-Cycle Content
For content that cannot wait for the standard calendar (e.g., rapid-response to industry news):
1. Email comms@huron-demo.ai with subject: `[URGENT] Content Request`
2. Provide a 2-3 sentence summary and target publication window
3. Communications will expedite within 24 hours for reactive social posts, 48 hours for written pieces
