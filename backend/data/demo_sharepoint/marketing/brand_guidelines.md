# Huron Brand Guidelines (Demo Document)
**Version:** 2026.1 | **Classification:** Internal | **Owner:** Corporate Communications & Marketing

---

## 1. Brand Promise

Huron's brand promise is: **"A new perspective. Lasting results."**

Every touchpoint — a proposal, a client email, a slide deck, a social post — should reinforce this promise. We are the firm that brings clarity to complexity and drives change that endures.

---

## 2. Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Attributes
| Attribute | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Expert** | We demonstrate mastery without jargon. We earn credibility through evidence, not credentials. |
| **Direct** | We say what we mean. No hedging, no corporate speak. Clear sentences, active verbs. |
| **Human** | We write like people, not institutions. We acknowledge complexity without hiding behind it. |
| **Optimistic** | We lead with possibility. We address challenges while keeping sight of what's achievable. |

### Tone Adjustments by Audience
- **C-Suite Clients:** Strategic, concise, evidence-backed. Avoid operational detail in opening pages.
- **Operational Clients:** Process-focused, practical, action-oriented. Show the "how."
- **Internal Communications:** Collegial but professional. Less formal than client materials.
- **Public / Media:** Measured, factual, spokesperson-reviewed before publishing.

### Words We Avoid
Do not use: synergy, leverage (as a verb), bandwidth (for human capacity), circle back, boil the ocean, low-hanging fruit, bleeding edge, move the needle (unless the context is literal).

## 3. Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
The Huron wordmark in Huron Blue (#0047AB) on a white background. Use this version as the default in all materials.

### Reverse Logo
White wordmark on Huron Blue background. Use only on dark backgrounds. Do not use on photographic backgrounds.

### Minimum Size
- Print: 1.5 inches wide minimum
- Digital: 120px wide minimum

### Clear Space
Maintain a clear space equal to the height of the "H" in "Huron" on all sides of the logo.

### Prohibited Logo Uses
- Do not stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
- Do not change logo colors (only approved color variants permitted)
- Do not place logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a color bar
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or effects
- Do not combine the Huron logo with a client or partner logo without written approval from Corporate Communications

- Do not use Huron Blue text on a dark background (low contrast)

- Do not introduce new colors without Corporate Communications approval

## 5. Typography

### Primary Typeface: Calibri (Office Default)
Used for all internal and client-deliverable documents created in Microsoft 365.

| Element | Font | Size | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Document Title | Calibri | 28pt | Bold |
| Section Header (H1) | Calibri | 20pt | Bold |
| Sub-Header (H2) | Calibri | 14pt | Bold |
| Body Text | Calibri | 11pt | Regular |
| Captions / Footnotes | Calibri | 9pt | Italic |
| Data Labels | Calibri | 9–11pt | Regular |

### Secondary Typeface: Georgia (for formal documents)
Used for formal reports, client proposals, and executive briefs.

Line spacing: 1.15 (standard) or 1.5 (for long-form reading documents). Never single-spaced for multi-paragraph content.

## 6. Slide Deck Standards

All client-facing decks must use the current Huron PowerPoint template (available on SharePoint → Marketing → Templates).

### Slide Structure Rules
- No more than 5 bullet points per slide
- One core idea per slide (the "so what" is always explicit)
- All slides must have a title (no untitled slides)
- Data must cite its source in a footnote
- Page numbers on all slides except cover and dividers

### Approved Image Sources
- Huron image library (SharePoint → Marketing → Images)
- Getty Images (via Huron enterprise license — see IT for access)
- Do not use Google Image Search, Unsplash, or AI-generated images in client materials without approval

## 7. External Communications Approval

All external-facing content must be approved before publication:

| Content Type | Approver |
|---|---|
| Press releases | VP Communications + General Counsel |
| Social media (LinkedIn, Twitter/X) | Social Media Manager |
| Client case studies | Client + VP of the relevant practice |
| Thought leadership articles | Practice Leader + Communications |
| Speaking proposals (external conferences) | Practice Leader |
| Client proposals (new business) | Engagement Director |

**Timeline:** Submit to Communications at comms@huron-demo.ai with 5 business days lead time for standard reviews. Complex content (legal implication, regulatory topics) may require up to 10 days.

---

## 8. Brand Violations

Misuse of the Huron brand — including unauthorized logo modification, off-brand color usage in client materials, or unauthorized external publications — must be corrected. Report violations to branding@huron-demo.ai. Repeated violations are escalated to the employee's manager.
