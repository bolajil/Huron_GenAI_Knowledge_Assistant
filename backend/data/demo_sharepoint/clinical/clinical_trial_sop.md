## 1. Purpose & Scope

This SOP defines Huron's standard approach for supporting clients with clinical trial management, regulatory compliance, and site operations. It applies to all Huron team members working on clinical trial advisory, site feasibility, protocol optimization, and trial operations engagements.

| Requirement | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|

## 3. Phase-Based Support Model

### Phase I — Protocol & Feasibility Support
**Objective:** Ensure the trial protocol is operationally feasible and regulatory-compliant before IND submission.

Key activities:
- Protocol synopsis review against FDA 21 CFR Parts 50, 56, 312 (or ICH E6 GCP for international)
- Site feasibility analysis: patient population, investigator experience, competing trials
- Enrollment forecasting model (historical enrollment rates + site capacity)
- Regulatory gap analysis against sponsor SOPs

**Deliverables:** Feasibility Report, Protocol Optimization Memo

### Phase II — Site Selection & Activation
**Objective:** Identify, qualify, and activate optimal clinical sites.

Key activities:
- Site qualification visit (SQV) checklist development
- Site contract template review (Clinical Trial Agreement, budget templates)
- IRB submission tracking dashboard
- Site activation timeline risk assessment

**Key Metric:** Site activation rate (target: ≥ 85% of selected sites activated on schedule)

### Phase III — Enrollment & Retention Support
**Objective:** Maximize enrollment velocity while maintaining protocol compliance.

Key activities:
- Enrollment dashboard design and implementation
- Patient recruitment strategy review (digital, community outreach, referral networks)
- Retention risk assessment and mitigation planning
- Protocol deviation trend analysis (identify systemic issues vs. site-specific)

**Key Metric:** Randomization rate vs. plan; screen failure rate by site

### Phase IV — Closeout & Regulatory Submission Support
**Objective:** Ensure clean, timely trial closeout and support regulatory submission preparation.

Key activities:
- Closeout visit planning and tracking
- Essential document binder audit (TMF reconciliation)
- Data cleaning and database lock coordination
- CSR (Clinical Study Report) outline review

## 4. Study Startup Checklist

1. Protocol review and IRB submission tracking
2. Site selection criteria documentation
3. Investigator Site File (ISF) setup — must achieve 100% completeness score
4. Training log maintenance (GCP + protocol-specific)
5. Pharmacy coordination for IMP storage and accountability

## 5. Data Management Standards

### Electronic Data Capture (EDC)
- All clinical data must be captured in the client's validated EDC system (Medidata Rave, Veeva Vault, Oracle InForm, REDCap, or equivalent)
- Huron does not operate its own EDC; team members access client systems under client-issued credentials
- Query resolution target: **5 business days** from generation (per ICH E6 GCP standard)

### Source Document Verification (SDV)
Risk-based monitoring (RBM) approach is the default per FDA 2023 guidance:
- 100% SDV for first patient at each site (learning visit)
- Risk-stratified SDV thereafter (typically 20–100% by data field risk tier)
- Remote SDV permitted for eSource sites with documented remote access agreement

### Data Retention
| Region | Retention Period (from trial completion) |
|---|---|
| United States (FDA) | 2 years after marketing application approval or withdrawal |
| European Union (EMA/EudraCT) | 25 years for medicinal products |
| ICH E6 (general) | At least 15 years |

## 6. Regulatory Reporting Requirements

| Event | Reporting Timeline | Recipient |
|---|---|---|
| Serious Adverse Event (SAE) | 24 hours to sponsor | Sponsor Safety Officer |
| Unexpected Serious Adverse Drug Reaction (SUSAR) | 7 days (fatal/life-threatening); 15 days (others) | FDA + IRB |
| IND Safety Report | 7/15 calendar days | FDA |
| Annual Report | 60 days before IND anniversary | FDA |
| Final Report | 12 months after study end | Sponsor + FDA |
| Protocol Deviation (Major) | Within 10 business days of detection | Sponsor + IRB |
| Protocol Amendment | Prior to implementation | FDA (if IND-impacting) + IRB |

Huron team members do not submit regulatory reports directly — reports are prepared in draft and submitted by the sponsor or CRO. Huron must flag events to the Engagement Director immediately upon detection.

## 7. Audit Readiness

- Sites must maintain 100% ISF completeness score at all times
- Huron team conducts monthly remote monitoring for all active sites
- All Huron-prepared deliverables require "DRAFT — NOT FOR REGULATORY USE" watermarks until final approval
- Engagement Document Index must be completed before project closeout

### Team Qualification Requirements
All Huron team members on clinical trial engagements must have:
- [ ] GCP training current (within 3 years; CITI Program or equivalent)
- [ ] HIPAA certification current (within 1 year)
- [ ] ICH E6(R3) guideline familiarity (2023 update — mandatory for all new engagements starting 2026)
